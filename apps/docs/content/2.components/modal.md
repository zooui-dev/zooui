`ZModal` is `UModal` with a 3px top edge in its `color`, Zoo UI's danger dialog. Focus trapping, Escape and focus return are Nuxt UI's, untouched.

::component-example{name="ModalEdge"}
::

The dialog portals to `<body>`, so with `color="accent"` it carries the `data-accent` of the section it was opened from.
