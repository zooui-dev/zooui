Verdict

This variant is narrower in application than the default: the mono-led interface is right for a console and wrong for a marketing site, and the 640px column wants a page with one thing on it. That is the point — it is a decision, not a setting. Keep the default for breadth; take this one when the product is a tool and you want it to look authored.
