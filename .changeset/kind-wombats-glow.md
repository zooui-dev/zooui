ZSelectMenu's typeahead searches from the top of the list, whatever is selected, and pressing one letter again and again cycles through the items that start with it. A typeahead pick now tells a surrounding UForm, so validate-on-change and dirty tracking see it, and v-model's modifiers apply to it. The trigger shows its focus ring on any focus, a click's included, not only on keyboard focus. The docs have a Coming from Buoy note.
