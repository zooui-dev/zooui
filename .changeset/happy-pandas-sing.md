New useConfirm(), ZConfirmDialog and ZConfirmButton. useConfirm() asks a question and awaits the answer: confirm() resolves true or false, choose() resolves to the picked action's value or null, and confirmDelete() ("Delete {what}", "Are you sure you want to delete {what}? This action cannot be undone.") and confirmDiscard() ("Unsaved Changes", with a red Discard and Keep Editing) are ready-made presets. A question takes a title, a description and details (a detail with a line break, or a { title, text }, shows a heading over its text), a color, confirmColor and confirmVariant for the confirming button (a red one makes the question destructive), dismissible: false (Escape and a click outside no longer cancel), and size ('sm', 'md' or 'lg'). An error or warning question shows its color's icon when it names none, and icon: false hides it. The questions render in ZConfirmDialog, which you mount once inside <UApp>. It's announced as an alertdialog, and Escape, the close button and a click outside all count as a cancel. The icon is a 40px badge in the body beside the description, so a question with only a title, or with details but no description, shows no icon; the footer has Cancel on the left and the answers on the right. On a destructive question, focus starts on Cancel. A question asked while another is open waits its turn, and dismissAll() clears the line. Each Nuxt app keeps its own queue, so nothing is shared between server requests, and on the server a call resolves at once as a cancel. ZConfirmButton is a UButton that asks "Are you sure?" in a popover, with an alert-circle icon before the question, an outlined red Yes, No, and an arrow; it emits confirm only on yes, and cancel otherwise. Every UButton prop passes through; the question, description and button labels are props, as are confirm-color, confirm-variant, question-icon (false for none) and arrow (false for none), and confirm-enabled="false" skips the question so a click emits confirm at once.
