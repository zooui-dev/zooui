useConfirm(), ZConfirmDialog and ZConfirmButton now do everything Buoy's confirm components did. confirm() takes confirmColor and confirmVariant for the confirming button (a warning dialog with a primary or a red button; a red one makes the question destructive), dismissible: false (Escape and a click outside no longer cancel), and size ('sm', 'md', 'lg'). An error or warning question shows its color's icon when it names none (icon: false hides it), and a detail with a line break, or a { title, text }, shows a heading over its text. ZConfirmButton adds confirm-enabled (false: no question, the click emits confirm), confirm-variant, question-icon and arrow. Defaults now follow Buoy: ZConfirmButton has an outlined Yes, the alert-circle icon before its question and an arrow, and no width cap; the dialog's footer has Cancel on the left and the answers on the right; confirmDelete asks "Delete {what}" with "Are you sure you want to delete {what}? This action cannot be undone."; confirmDiscard asks about "Unsaved Changes" with a red Discard and Keep Editing. The docs have a Coming from Buoy table: Buoy's confirmLabel on UConfirmButton was the question, and here confirm-label is the Yes button.
